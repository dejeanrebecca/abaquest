## Scene 31
![Scene 31](./quest4_scene31.svg)

### Content
- Story mode (Practice-Adding 0)
- Dev. Instructions
The user can tap the junior counter he/she will help or by moving beads on the counter to the correct position.
Each tap or drag should move one bead up at a time.
When the number of beads matches the math problem (for example, 0 + 4 = 4 beads up), the system should recognize it as correct.
- Feedback: (in notes)

### Narrator Script (Cousin's Voice)
> What happens when they get it right
When the user fixes a student’s counter correctly:
Play a happy chime sound
Show a green check mark or a sparkle on that student
Play Abby’s voice:
“Yes! That’s right — one plus zero is one!”or“Nice helping! That student has the right amount now.”
Give the user 1 Quest Coin
Lock that student’s counter so it can’t be changed
When all 6 students are correct, play a group celebration:
“You helped everyone! Great teamwork!”


What happens if they make a mistake
If the user adds too many beads or not enough:
Play a soft, gentle error sound (not a buzzer)
Shake or wiggle the counter slightly to show it’s wrong
Have Abby say something encouraging, like:
“Hmm, that’s a little too many. Try again.”
“You don’t need that many beads.”
“Check the number in the problem — it’s zero plus four.”
If they try three times and still can’t get it:
Gently highlight the correct counter position
Abby says:
“Here’s what it should look like — four beads for zero plus four!”

## Scene 32
![Scene 32](./quest4_scene32.svg)

### Content
- Story mode (Practice-Adding 0)
- 1
- 0
- 2
- “Use your junior counter to solve each problem. 
Move the beads to find the answer, then tap or drag the number that shows what you found!”

### Narrator Script (Cousin's Voice)
> Developer Flow
1. When the screen loads
Play Abby’s instruction VO above.
Junior counter starts empty (no beads up).
User can move beads up or down freely.
User must pick or drag the correct answer to each problem’s blank circle.

2. When the user selects the correct answer
(For example, choosing “0” for 0+0 or “2” for 0+2)
Actions:
Play a soft “✨chime” sound (SFX_SUCCESS_CHIME).
Highlight the correct circle with a soft green glow.
Show a quick sparkle or “pop” animation.
Lock that question so it can’t be changed.
Reward: add 1 Quest Coin to the counter (optional).
Abby VO options (rotate randomly):
“Nice work! That’s right — zero plus zero is zero.”
“Good job! You got it — one plus zero is one.”
“Great thinking! Zero plus two makes two.”
“Perfect! You’re using your counter like a real mathematician.”

3. When the user picks the wrong answer
Actions:
Play a gentle “oops” sound (SFX_SOFT_ERROR).
Shake or wiggle the wrong circle briefly.
Keep the question active (don’t lock it yet).
Show a light red outline that fades away in 1–2 seconds.
Abby VO options (rotate randomly):
“Hmm, not quite. Try using your counter to check again.”
“Let’s count your beads — does that match your answer?”
“Almost! Move your beads and see what number you get.”

Hints (optional after 2 incorrect tries)
If the user misses the same problem twice:
Highlight the correct number with a soft pulsing glow.
Play Abby VO hint:
“Here’s a tip — move your beads to match the problem. Zero plus two means, freeze the beads to position to zero and move two beads up!”

## Scene 36
![Scene 36](./quest4_scene36.svg)

### Content
- “Now let’s see what this looks like on your junior counter. Help the students correctly add one or two beads to the position of the junior counter next to them.
- Story mode (Practice-Adding 2)

### Narrator Script (Cousin's Voice)
> Developer Flow
1. On Screen Load
Play Abby’s instruction VO above.
Show all six students, each with a visible junior counter and math problem above their heads.
The counters should show only the first number of each problem (for example, for 1+2, start with 1 bead up).
All problems are interactive — students can be solved in any order.

User Interaction
The user moves beads upward on each counter to show the total.
Each counter listens for the correct number of beads (the sum).

When the user gets it right- by placing the correct bead positioning
Actions:
Play success sound → SFX_SUCCESS_CHIME.
Highlight the correct counter with a soft green glow.
Show a quick sparkle or pop animation on that student.
Lock that counter (no more movement).
Reward: +1 Quest Coin.
Play Abby’s positive feedback VO.
Abby VO options (rotate randomly):
“Yes! One plus two makes three — nice work!”
“That’s right! Two plus one also makes three.”
“You got it! Zero plus two equals two.”
“Excellent! Two plus two is four — you’re getting good at this!”

❌ When the user moves beads incorrectly
Actions:
Play gentle “oops” sound → SFX_SOFT_ERROR.
Counter shakes slightly (0.3s).
Fade in/out a red outline around the counter (no harsh flash).
Keep the counter unlocked so they can fix it.
Abby VO options:
“Hmm, that doesn’t look right. Try counting again.”
“Check your counter — does it match the problem?”
“Almost there! Move one more bead and see what happens.”

Hint Behavior (optional after 2 wrong tries)
If the learner gets it wrong twice:
Highlight the correct number of beads with a glowing guide animation.
Play Abby’s hint VO:
“Try counting all the beads together — one, two, three. That’s the answer!”

🎉 When all problems are correct
Actions:
Play celebration sound → SFX_CELEBRATION_SHORT.
Show sparkles around all six students.
Display +6 Quest Coins (if using reward system).
Play Abby’s closing VO.
